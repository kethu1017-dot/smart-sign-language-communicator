ℹ️ How It Works

  1. Open the live camera and allow camera permission.
  2. The hand-tracking library identifies hand landmarks.
  3. Basic finger-position rules estimate a gesture.
  4. The app displays an English word and Tamil meaning.
  5. Text-to-voice reads a message aloud.

This is a demonstration. Reliable recognition of real sign language requires a trained model and testing with a suitable dataset.
