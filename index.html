How It Works

Simple communication flow of the Smart Sign Language Communicator.

1️⃣ 🖐 Input

Use the live camera or upload a sign-language video.

2️⃣ 🧠 Detection

MediaPipe detects hand, face and body landmarks.

3️⃣ 🤖 AI Recognition

The recognition layer maps the gesture to a sign word.

4️⃣ 🔊 Communication

The result is shown as text and can be spoken aloud.
